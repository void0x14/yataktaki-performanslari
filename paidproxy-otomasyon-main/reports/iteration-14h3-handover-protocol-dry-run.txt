## Stale transitional reconciliation preview

PID 64117 belongs to persisted ajan-10c9647a6eb94b50 and is recorded as stopping, but /proc/64117/stat is absent. It was omitted from the live manifest because no process exists. During a future successful supervisor handover, the new supervisor will conservatively mark this non-adopted record unknown and append recovery_required with previous state/PID/PGID in handover_reconciliation. It will fail startup instead if that exact PID is present.

No state mutation was made during this dry-run.
